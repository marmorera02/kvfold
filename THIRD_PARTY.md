Everything else in `data/` is generated by the scripts in `src/` (synthetic supply agreements, authored needle clauses)
or is original text. Outputs derived from the Wikipedia articles, such as corpora with planted clauses, summaries and
scored pairs, are not distributed; regenerate them with `src/e2_fourneedle/gen_corpus_wiki.py` and `run_phase_e.py`.
The result tables in `results/` quote a few sentences from those articles in their diagnostic discussion.

Software: llama-cpp-python, sentence-transformers, transformers, PyTorch (CPU), spaCy, zstandard, NumPy, Matplotlib
(pinned in `requirements.txt`).
